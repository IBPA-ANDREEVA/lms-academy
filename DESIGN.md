# LMS frontend design context

Russian-first beauty education. Public discovery uses restrained editorial composition; forms and protected workspaces use quiet functional layouts. Product facts and release scope override decorative references. Read [design guidance](docs/design/README.md), [decisions](docs/design/DECISIONS.md), and the [frontend registry](docs/architecture/frontend-components.md).

Tokens: `docs/design/SYSTEM.md` → `src/app/globals.css` → component CSS modules. Manrope is functional type; Unbounded is short display type, opt-in only. Both are self-hosted through `next/font/local` from `src/assets/fonts`, with their original OFL licenses. No Geist, Arial, synthetic weights, dark-mode auto-recoloring, or third-party font requests.

Container widths and the three button families follow the preserved owner-edited definitions in the registry. Header geometry follows the image supplied in this chat on 2026-10-28 (desktop, dropdown, sticky, tablet and mobile states). The rounded header and pill account action are scoped exceptions, not global geometry. Footer follows the final dark direction. Owner-requested preview navigation is populated; unavailable destinations are labeled «Скоро» and have no href. No legal text or unpublished route is fabricated. Other component styles are bounded token-based proposals, not approved designs.

The homepage contains HOME-02/03, the HOME-05 local learning demo, and the owner-requested HOME-06–09 horizontal learning-offers group. Its section switcher is sticky only within that group, below the existing PublicHeader. Purple geometry crosses section boundaries through zero-height seam owners outside section clipping. HOME-06–09 share static top/bottom geometry outside the animated track; switching changes content, background color and natural active height, not the shape. There is no right-side decorative rail. Compact panel headings and tighter section spacing preserve one continuous page. Packages and Start in USA are visibly marked previews, not released offers; all course inventory remains labeled placeholders. The updated hero reference scopes a transparent PublicHeader over the photograph on `/`; it becomes a smaller rounded white sticky bar with side gutters shortly after scrolling starts. Hero fills at least the viewport, and its CTA connector lines are removed. The shared Cut button follows the supplied parallelogram outline and detached trailing slash; this does not change the other two button families. Other routes retain the white rounded header. Original photographs in `public/ibpa/` are unchanged. No prices, checkout, live package policy or unpublished route were invented. Implementation status and remaining work live in `docs/architecture/frontend-*.md`.
