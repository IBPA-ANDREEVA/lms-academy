# Screen Composition Specs

This document describes hierarchy and user goals, not pixel-perfect design. Confirm all features against the approved academy requirements; do not invent policies, prices or content.

## Marketing homepage
**Goal:** explain value and support course discovery.
**Order:** distinctive hero + clear course CTA; six directions; featured real course programs; the selective interactive learning preview; tutor credibility and practical benefits; relevant footer/legal.
**Style:** expressive, open, photography or modular geometry when meaningful. Avoid default three-identical-card section repetition.
**Primary action:** view courses.
**Mobile:** headline wraps naturally, CTA visible, demo is usable without registration.

## Catalog
**Goal:** compare/find the right program.
**Core:** direction, language, level filters; course image, language availability, level, curator support, price, useful summary.
**Style:** structured and scannable, with controlled editorial variation. Filters are functional rather than decorative.
**States:** empty results, loading, unavailable course, filter reset.

## Course detail
**Goal:** understand curriculum, instructor, support and price before purchase.
**Core:** title, outcomes, lesson structure, preview, language options, tutor qualifications, price, policies, purchase CTA.
**Optional signature:** course-specific demo; avoid repeating full homepage demo.
**Mobile:** CTA and purchase terms remain legible, no hidden necessary conditions.

## Student dashboard
**Goal:** resume learning quickly.
**Priority:** next lesson; pending assignments and deadlines; new curator feedback; purchased courses and progress; certificates; optional recommendations.
**Style:** quiet functional lists and selective course imagery.
**States:** new enrollment, in progress, completed course, tutor support expired.

## Lesson screen
**Goal:** watch, study, submit practical work and understand review outcome.
**Core:** video, speed/language/subtitles, lesson sequence, materials, assignment upload preview, written feedback, curator support remaining.
**Critical:** photo/video uploads require visible preview and clear upload status; do not visually hide limitations or due dates.

## Trainer review queue
**Goal:** efficiently prioritize and review student submissions.
**Core:** sorted queue, course/lesson, student, remaining or overdue response time, media preview, comment/voice feedback, approve or revision action.
**Style:** dense but readable, status is not color-only.
**Privacy:** no unnecessary student contact exposure.

## Manager and admin
**Goal:** operate catalog, users, enrollments, sales, refunds, commissions and reports.
**Style:** predictable tables and forms, strong permission and action clarity, minimal decorative signatures.
**Critical:** do not imply access or permissions that aren't in the actual role model.

## Screen definition template
For new screens document: primary user and goal, content hierarchy, main action, error/empty/loading/success states, desktop/mobile composition, applicable signatures (or none), real data dependencies, approved reference link, QA checks.
